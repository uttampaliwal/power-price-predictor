\textbf{Cover Letter}

Dear Editor,

I would like to submit my manuscript ``Day-Ahead Electricity Price Forecasting in the Indian Energy Exchange: Gradient-Boosted Ensembles at 15-Minute Resolution'' for consideration in Applied Energy.

This paper presents a systematic empirical study of machine learning models for day-ahead electricity price forecasting on the Indian Energy Exchange (IEX), evaluated at the exchange's native 15-minute resolution. Using approximately 2.2 million block-level observations spanning January 2020--May 2026, we benchmark five model families under a strict 17-month temporal holdout.

\textbf{Key contributions:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item First large-scale EPF benchmark on Indian market data at 15-minute resolution with a 17-month holdout horizon (substantially longer than typical in the literature).
  \item Weather ablation showing that city-level temperature features add no measurable value ($|\Delta R^2| \le 0.004$) in day-ahead auctions where participants' bids already embed weather intelligence.
  \item Evidence that directional skill (AUC-ROC) decouples from level accuracy (RMSE), with practical implications for objective-specific model selection.
  \item Full open-source pipeline: https://github.com/uttampaliwal/power-price-predictor
\end{itemize}

This work has not been published elsewhere and is not under consideration by another journal. The author has approved the manuscript and agrees with its submission to Applied Energy.
